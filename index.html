SizeSure — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

SizeSure ("the app") is a utility for preparing photos, signatures and documents for online forms. This policy explains what happens to your data. It applies to the Android and iOS apps.

Summary

SizeSure processes your photos, signatures and documents entirely on your device. We do not run a server, and we never upload, collect or store your files anywhere else.

Files you process

  • Images and PDFs you select are read only to perform the action you choose (compress, resize, crop, convert).
  • Results are stored in the app's private storage so they appear in History. You can delete them at any time, clear all history, or turn History off in Settings.
  • Files are copied to your gallery / Photos / Downloads / Files only when you tap Save, and shared only when you tap Share.
  • Location and camera metadata (EXIF, including GPS) is removed from images SizeSure creates.

Permissions

  • Photos: SizeSure uses the system photo picker, so it can see only the photos you choose. It never requests access to your whole library.
  • Camera (optional): used only when you choose to photograph a signature or a passport photo.
  • Save to Photos (iOS, add-only) / Storage (Android 9 and older): requested only when you save a file.
  • Internet: used only to show ads and to process the optional Remove Ads purchase. Your files are never sent over the network.

Advertising

SizeSure is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address and ad-interaction data to show, limit and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's certified consent tool before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS, personalised ads require your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Ads never have access to your photos or documents.

Purchases

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Analytics

SizeSure does not send analytics or crash reports to any server.

Children

SizeSure is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
