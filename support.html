Frequently asked questions

How do I make a photo an exact size, like 50 KB?

Open Compress Photo (or tap a size under Popular Sizes), pick your photo and choose the size. FormReady finds the highest quality that fits and never goes over the limit.

The form says the photo must be between two sizes (for example 20–50 KB).

On the Compress screen, turn on Minimum size and enter the lower limit.

Does FormReady upload my photos or documents?

No. Everything is processed on your phone. FormReady has no server. See the privacy policy.

Is the passport photo officially accepted?

FormReady prepares the size and framing. Rules for background, expression and lighting differ by country, so always check the official requirements.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account you used to buy it.

Where are my saved files?

iPhone: Photos app (images) or the Files location you chose (PDFs). Android: Gallery › Pictures/FormReady (images) and Downloads/FormReady (PDFs).
